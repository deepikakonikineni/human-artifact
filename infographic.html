Technology & the workplace

Used, but hidden

AI has moved into daily work faster than most workplaces have figured out what to do about it. The more interesting number isn't how many people use it — it's how many don't say so.

58% of employees worldwide say they intentionally use AI at work.
Doesn't use AI Uses AI openly Uses AI, hides it

Why the concealment?

The same global study points to a workplace that hasn't caught up to how its people are already working:

31% use AI weekly or daily — this isn't occasional use
47% have received any formal AI training
40% say their workplace has an actual AI policy

Most employees using AI at work are doing it without training or a policy to follow — and about half say they're worried about falling behind if they don't use it. Concealment, in that light, looks less like dishonesty and more like people managing a risk nobody has given them rules for.

Sources

Nicole Gillespie & Steve Lockey (Melbourne Business School, University of Melbourne), in collaboration with KPMG International, Trust, Attitudes and Use of Artificial Intelligence: A Global Study 2025 — survey of over 48,000 people across 47 countries, Nov 2024–Jan 2025.
